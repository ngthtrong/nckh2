\FormSection{2. Objective(s):}
Develop a mobile AI system that receives and analyzes flood rescue reports to support decisions under unstable network conditions.

\FormSection{3. Creativeness and innovativeness:}
The prototype combines edge image inference, adaptive transmission and event clustering. Location, time and report content support grouping and verification on a map.

\FormSection{4. Research results:}
On 256 test images, the seed-1024 baseline reached \NewImageAccuracy{}\% accuracy and \NewImageMacroFOne{}\% macro-F1, with three critical errors. Historical pruning reached \PruningAccuracy{}\% accuracy (McNemar \(p=0.1173\)). A 5/10/20\% validation grid selected 5\%; test macro-F1 fell to \LocalPrunedFOne{}\%. Benchmarks covered 32 configurations on four phones. The CPU image tests used artificial degradations.

Offline, English CrisisMMD text/fusion macro-F1 was \MMTextFOne{}\%/\MMFusionFOne{}\%. The Vietnamese translation and AI-label pilot used \VISilverTrainN/\VISilverDevN/\VISilverTestN{} train/dev/test pairs. Fusion reached \VISilverFusionFOne{}\% macro-F1 and \VISilverFusionRecall{}\% request recall. DistilBERT fine-tuning averaged \VISilverFineFOne{}\% macro-F1 over three seeds. Neither pilot established a fusion advantage.

\begin{samepage}
In a computer-based proxy test at 50 kbit/s and 600 ms round-trip time, metadata and compressed images succeeded in 257/257 attempts, versus 230/257 (89.49\%) for originals.\par
\end{samepage}

RQ1 does not establish Product superiority over independently selected Additive (ARI 0.9072 versus 0.9191; Holm \(p=0.2560\)). RQ2 supports exact-copy invariance under fixed grouping without general alignment gains (NDCG@5 0.6669 versus random 0.6763). RQ3 favors nearest-first on simulated harm and deadline misses.

\FormSection{5. Products:}
The registered products are an AI-assisted rescue app, a web dashboard for mapping reports and clustering events, and a mobile-optimized multimodal AI model package. Other outputs are a research bulletin, a summary and a demonstration video of up to two minutes.

``Stress-Testing Product-Gated Clustering and Bounded Priority Ranking for Flood-Rescue Reports: A Synthetic Study'' was published online on 13 November 2026 in ISDS 2026, Part II, CCIS vol.~3253, pp.~\ProjectPublicationPages. DOI~\ProjectPublicationDOI.
