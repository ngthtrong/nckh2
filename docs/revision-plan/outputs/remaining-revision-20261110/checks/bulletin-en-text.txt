                 SUMMARY REPORT RESEARCH PROJECT
− Project code: THS2026-68
− Project title: Development of an Edge AI-based System for Multimodal Analysis and Clustering of
  Flood Rescue Events
− Project period: May–October 2026
− Total cost: 15,000,000 VND
− Project leader: Lê Thị Ngọc Ảnh (0376932994)
− Project members: Nguyễn Thanh Trọng, Cao Tường Hưng, Nguyễn Như Quỳnh, Ngô Hưng Thịnh.
− Advisor: TS. Nguyễn Thanh Khoa (ntkhoa@ctu.edu.vn)
− Necessity of the project: Duplicate, unlocated or delayed rescue reports hinder coordination under
  intermittent connectivity.
− Objectives: Develop a prototype combining edge image inference, compact transmission and map-
  based event clustering.
− Methodology: Train MobileNetV3-Large and evaluate image classification, clustering, ranking and
  dispatch separately using saved artifacts and simulation datasets.
− Project activities: Image AI, structured urgency, persistent queues, adaptive sending, synchroniza-
  tion and dashboard. A translated-Vietnamese/AI-silver pilot runs NLP and offline fusion; native
  data, information extraction and camera/Android evaluation remain open.
− Research results: The baseline reaches 75.78% accuracy and 74.26% macro-F1 on 256 images,
  with three critical errors; phone benchmarks cover 32 configurations on four devices. McNemar
  does not establish historical pruning gains; a 5/10/20% grid does not preserve quality. PTQ/QAT
  diagnostics remain partial. The app, backend and dashboard exist; the ranking adapter does not use
  LR scores. English CrisisMMD fusion reaches 78.35% macro-F1 with effect CIs including zero.
  On AI-translated/AI-silver text, fusion reaches 64.91% macro-F1 and 33.33% request recall; CUDA
  T2 averages 52.29% macro-F1. Independent gold remains open; mobile NLP/fusion integration is
  confirmed by the team.
− Research new finding: RQ1 does not establish Product superiority; RQ2 does not establish general
  alignment gains and retains three raw Spearman and five Holm replay differences. RQ3 favors
  nearest-first over revised priority on simulated harm and deadline misses. Field rescue benefits
  remain unmeasured.
− Application potentials: Research and workflow demonstration. Urgency integration, mobile prove-
  nance, repeated device/network/SMS tests and expert validation remain necessary.
