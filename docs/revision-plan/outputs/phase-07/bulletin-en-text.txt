                 SUMMARY REPORT RESEARCH PROJECT
− Project code: THS2026-68
− Project title: Development of an Edge AI-based System for Multimodal Analysis and Clustering of
  Flood Rescue Events
− Project period: May–October 2026
− Total cost: 15,000,000 VND
− Project leader: Lê Thị Ngọc Ảnh (0376932994)
− Project members: Nguyễn Thanh Trọng, Cao Tường Hưng, Nguyễn Như Quỳnh, Ngô Hưng Thịnh.
− Advisor: TS. Nguyễn Thanh Khoa (ntkhoa@ctu.edu.vn)
− Necessity of the project: Duplicate, unlocated or delayed rescue reports hinder coordination under
  intermittent connectivity.
− Objectives: Develop a prototype combining edge image inference, compact transmission and map-
  based event clustering.
− Methodology: Train MobileNetV3-Large and evaluate image classification, clustering, ranking and
  dispatch separately using saved artifacts and simulation datasets.
− Project activities: Image AI, structured urgency, persistent queues, adaptive sending, synchroniza-
  tion and dashboard development; conditional edge and copy-invariance audits.
− Research results: The original seed-1024 model achieves 75.78% accuracy and 74.26% macro-F1
  on 256 test images in CPU evaluation, with three critical errors. Runtime conversion, quantization
  and pruning were evaluated separately; quantization reduced model size but also accuracy. Phone
  benchmarks cover 32 configurations on four devices. A Flutter app, backend and dashboard were
  developed. The five-flag LR is not free-text NLP; its score is not consumed by the current ranking
  adapter. Learned fusion remains unfulfilled.
− Research new finding: RQ1 does not establish Product superiority; RQ2 does not establish gen-
  eral alignment gains and has unresolved replay differences. RQ3 favors nearest-first over revised
  priority on simulated harm and deadline misses. Field rescue benefits remain unmeasured.
− Application potentials: Research and workflow demonstration. Urgency integration, mobile prove-
  nance, repeated device/network/SMS tests and expert validation remain necessary.
