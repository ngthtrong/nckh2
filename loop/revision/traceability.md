## Manuscript contract

Every empirical number in `paper/main.tex` must be emitted as `\RevisionClaim{claim.id}` from `paper/generated/revision_results.tex`. The final audit resolves every used ID against `claim-selectors.json`; a literal, unregistered result is a Gate-4 failure. Mathematical constants, equation labels, citation years, and explicitly identified protocol constants are audited separately against their source files.

No selector points to a rejected run. Negative, tied, adverse, failed, and infeasible outcomes remain represented in the locked source and promotion audit.
