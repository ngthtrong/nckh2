# Text Label Schema

## urgent_rescue
Definition:
- Immediate life risk and urgent rescue request.

Indicators:
- cuu voi, ket trong nha, nuoc dang noc, tre em, nguoi gia, bi co lap.

## need_supplies
Definition:
- Needs support items but not immediate life-threatening state.

Indicators:
- can gao, can thuoc, can ao phao, mat dien, het thuc an.

## safe_update
Definition:
- Safety status update or non-urgent local update.

Indicators:
- da so tan an toan, nuoc da rut, tinh hinh on dinh.

## irrelevant
Definition:
- Not related to actionable rescue coordination.

Indicators:
- weather commentary without request, ads, unrelated discussion.
